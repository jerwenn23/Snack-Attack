About Snack Attack

Snack Attack is a small snack business for students. We sell crispy sweet potato fries, cheesy fries, and other potato snacks, plus cold lemonades. Order online, pay with GCash, Maya or cash, and pick it up outside Punta Princesa Elementary & Night School in Dela Cerna.

Quick answers

How do I order?

Open the Menu, tap Order now on a snack, pick your size and flavor, then add it to your cart. Open your cart, check out, and choose how you want to pay.

Where do I pick up my order?

We are outside Punta Princesa Elementary & Night School, Dela Cerna. Open in Google Maps. It is pickup only.

When are you open?

Every day, 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

Which payments do you accept?

GCash or Maya (scan our QR code at checkout), or cash when you pick up.

How do I pay with GCash or Maya?

Pick GCash or Maya at checkout, scan the QR code with your app, type the amount shown, and send. Then enter the reference number from your receipt and tap I’ve paid. We check your payment before preparing your order.

Can I choose the size or flavor?

Yes. Plain fries come in Small, Medium and Large with your choice of flavor, and some snacks have sauces and add-ons.
